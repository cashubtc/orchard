Waiting for the coordinator to propose

Your mint enrolls in its assigned slot. Once every operator has joined, the coordinator publishes the proposal.

Your identity fingerprint appears as soon as you join. Read it to the other operators over a call or chat you trust; they'll check it against the proposal before approving.
